# Sample image sources

Every image in this folder is in the public domain or dedicated to it (CC0),
checked on its source page before it was added. Authors are credited as a
courtesy; neither license requires it.

All files were re-saved without metadata (EXIF, XMP, IPTC, comments).
Files are sized by prefix. `l-` files are at most 2048 px on the long edge;
larger originals were downscaled. `m-` files are at most 1600 px: smaller
scans, and vector drawings rendered to that size.

## Adding an image

- **License:** only CC0 or public domain, confirmed on the source page
  itself. Don't rely on search-engine or aggregator labels, and skip
  anything with a watermark.
- **Content:** no identifiable real person as the main subject, and no
  copyrighted artwork inside the picture (book covers, posters, screens).
  No demeaning caricatures or stereotypes of people, which rules out a lot of
  public-domain material from the early 1900s. Review the whole image at full
  size, not just a thumbnail.
- **Files:** strip all metadata before committing, and keep files to at
  most 2048 px on the long edge.
