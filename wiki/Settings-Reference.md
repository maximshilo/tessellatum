# Settings reference

Every setting in the sidebar, by page and section, as the app defines it. This page is generated from the settings registry (`src/tessellatum/core/settings.py`) by `docs/make_settings_reference.py`, and a test fails when it falls behind; what each setting is *for*, with examples, is in the [User guide](User-Guide#settings).

- **Level**: *Shown first* (the choices most pages need), *Worth a look*, or *Advanced* (folded away in the page's Advanced group). See [Levels of detail](User-Guide#levels-of-detail).
- **Applied**: *with Generate* (it makes the page again), *drawn again by itself* (it only draws the page again from its regions, a moment after the last change; Preferences sets how soon), or *at once* (it changes only what is drawn over the page).
- **Sizes** are on the printed page, on the paper chosen. Colors differ in CIEDE2000 (ΔE00: about 1 is the smallest difference an eye sees side by side); grays are a lightness, L* 0 (black) to 100 (white).
- **Presets**: the difficulty presets set the settings under Generation > Detail, Faces and subject, Line art and Text, and the Colors page's palette. Moving one makes the difficulty Custom.

## Where they are kept

The settings shown are kept between runs, each under its key (`settings/<name>` for the sliders; the choices' keys are `difficulty/…`, `style/…` and `handling/…`), in the platform's settings store: the registry on Windows, a `.plist` on macOS, an `.ini` file on Linux. A project file keeps them with its picture instead (see [Projects](User-Guide#projects)).
