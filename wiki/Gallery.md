# Gallery

Each strip shows the original picture, its coloring page and the page painted in its legend colors. Every page was made
by the current version with the difficulty named under it; the pictures are the benchmark images, all CC0 or public
domain ([sources](https://github.com/maximshilo/tessellatum/blob/main/tests/sample_images/SOURCES.md)).

## Photographs

**A lion.** Fur becomes smooth, brush-sized patches, and the face and the subject get finer regions.
![Lion](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maximshilo/tessellatum/main/docs/gallery/photo-lion.jpg)

**A cat.** Face detection works on animals too: the eyes and nose keep regions of their own while the background stays broad.
![Cat](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maximshilo/tessellatum/main/docs/gallery/photo-cat.jpg)

**A castle on a lake.** Reflections and clouds settle into a few broad shapes.
![Oberhofen castle](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maximshilo/tessellatum/main/docs/gallery/photo-castle.jpg)

**Architecture at Hard.** A finer brush and smaller regions for a page with more to paint.
![Palermo castle](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maximshilo/tessellatum/main/docs/gallery/photo-architecture.jpg)

**A sunset.** A smooth gradient becomes a few broad bands rather than thin slivers.
![Sunset](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maximshilo/tessellatum/main/docs/gallery/photo-sunset.jpg)

## Paintings

**A portrait.** Face detection keeps the eyes, nose and mouth, and the skin settles into a few large tones.
![Portrait](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maximshilo/tessellatum/main/docs/gallery/painting-portrait.jpg)

## Line art

**A bold-line cartoon.** The ink is printed in black and the palette is the artist's own fill colors.
![Cartoon](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maximshilo/tessellatum/main/docs/gallery/cartoon-line-art.jpg)

**A cartoon in grays.** Flat gray fills, each one region.
![Cartoon in grays](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maximshilo/tessellatum/main/docs/gallery/cartoon-grays.jpg)

**A comic page.** Captions and lettered signs are detected and printed; hatching is painted over.
![Comic page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maximshilo/tessellatum/main/docs/gallery/comic-text.jpg)

**A scanned illustration.** Printed ink and dense hatching from a 1910 print.
![Scanned illustration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maximshilo/tessellatum/main/docs/gallery/illustration-scan.jpg)
